## 0. Verdict (owner-first)

**Not a tight isomorphism. Not a category error either. The shared object is a constraint, not a 3×3 table.** `analysis`

Marr’s three levels, the Hub stack (description / mechanism / ontology), and the four-layer information discriminant are **three different kinds of stratification**:

| Framework | Kind of object | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Marr (1982) | Levels of *analysis of a given information-processing system* | How do we explain a system that already exists and has a function? |
| Hub stack | Layers of an *account of reality* | What are we compressing, what transitions, what exists? |
| Four-layer info test | A *warrant ladder* for one claim-family | How far has a “X is information / code” claim actually climbed? |

What *does* transfer — and is worth locking — is an **anti-smuggling homomorphism**: answers to question *n* are not answers to question *m*. That is the durable content of the owner intuition, and it is already sedimented from the Red Pill / Closer To Truth scan. `sediment`

Calling the three columns “the same three layers” would **re-introduce** the failure mode the YT metabolism was built to stop. The most dangerous cell in the v0 table is **“Implementational = Ontology + medium.”** That cell glues two questions the four-layer test was invented to keep apart. `analysis`

Corrected slogan, if one is needed:

> Marr organizes *how we analyze a functioning system*. The Hub stack organizes *what kind of claim we are making*. The four-layer test organizes *how much warrant an informationalist claim has*. Shared discipline: no cross-layer smuggling. Not shared: the inventory of layers.

## 1. What each framework actually is

### 1.1 Marr’s levels (Vision, 1982)

Marr’s published triad, not the STT four-word list:

1. **Computational theory** — What is the *goal* of the computation? Why is that goal appropriate? What is the logic of the strategy? (what / why)
2. **Representation and algorithm** — What is the representation of input and output, and what algorithm transforms one into the other? (how, including encoding)
3. **Hardware implementation** — How can that representation and algorithm be realized physically? (in what stuff)

Three facts about Marr that the v0 table under-weights:

- **Representation is level 2, not level 1.** Marr’s middle name is literally “representation and algorithm.” The computational level is a *problem specification*, not a picture of the world. `hard_result`
- **Marr presupposes a system with a function.** Vision “produces from images of the external world a description that is useful to the viewer.” The computational level is relative to a consumer. A cosmos has no such consumer unless one is smuggled in. `hard_result`
- **Marr is methodologically functionalist.** Same computational theory, many algorithms, many hardwares. The implementational level is a *realization slot*, not an answer to “what exists.” Mapping it to ontology inverts Marr: he *brackets* being in order to study function. `analysis`

Owner STT (“Massey” + 物理 / 实现 / 算法 / 表征) listed **four** Chinese words for Marr’s **three** levels. 物理 ≈ 实现 is one Marr level (implementational). 算法 + 表征 is the middle level. The computational/what-why level is the one the STT list dropped — and the v0 table then filled that hole with “description / representational.” That is the seed of the misalignment. `analysis`

### 1.2 Hub stack (description / mechanism / ontology)

As used on the YT metabolism and named in the v0 crosswalk:

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Compress phenomena; state invariants and the goal of the *account* |
| **Mechanism** | Transition rules. Incomplete without the render 4-tuple: (trigger, state params, transition, readout). Incomplete 4-tuple ⇒ metaphor only. |
| **Ontology** | What exists / what the world is made of |

Theory mainline is the ontology question: what the universe fundamentally is. Consciousness, self-boundary, hysteresis, digital-subject engineering are subdomains, not substitutes. `sediment`

Two already-standing constraints belong *inside* this stack, not beside it:

- **先排除表示、控制参数和读出偷换，再谈机制与本体.** Representation, control-parameter, and readout substitutions are to be excluded *before* mechanism and ontology are discussed. (Rate–amplitude / gain-separation line.) `sediment`
- **Engineering vocabulary does not reach ultimate ontology.** Noun-grammar that works at the mechanism layer (state, loop, channel, projection) is not licensed as a 胜义 / ultimate-ontology answer. `sediment`

### 1.3 Four-layer information discriminant (orthogonal axis)

Warrant ladder for claims of the form “X is information / code / computation”:

1. **Describable** — a representation can be written down
2. **Compressible generative law** — a shorter rule regenerates the phenomena
3. **Causal execution in a medium** — the rule is a transition that happens *in something*, with energy, constraints, and failure modes
4. **Information-as-ontology** — the claim that information (or code, or computation) *is what exists*

Already sedimented: no cross-layer smuggling; new entities (simulator, frequency, karmic database, …) must pay an explanation-gain bar (compression, unification, or discriminating prediction). `sediment`

The encoding → physical-possibility increment is the same ladder in other words: *reality can be encoded* only licenses rung 1; it does not select laws or ontology. An information ontology still owes an account of state, measurement probabilities, composition, and allowed change. Quantum reconstruction is an entry, not a proof that “information creates matter.” `sediment`

Do not collapse that public note’s four *premises* (可编码 / 可压缩 / 可执行 / 物理实现) with this warrant ladder’s four *rungs*. The note’s fourth item is still the medium/realization slot. The ladder’s fourth rung is the informationalist *ontological claim*. Mixing them is H3/X3. `analysis`

**Orthogonality is load-bearing.** Marr-ish levels organize analysis of a system; the four-layer test disciplines a claim-family. Folding them into one column is a new smuggling route. `analysis`

## 2. What “isomorphism” would have to mean

An isomorphism is not “we can draw a table with three rows.” It requires a structure-preserving bijection with an inverse. `hard_result`

If the Marr ↔ Hub map were an isomorphism, all of the following would hold:

1. Each Marr level has exactly one Hub-stack image, and conversely.
2. Marr’s downward multiple-realizability (one function, many algorithms, many hardwares) maps to a corresponding Hub relation.
3. A well-posed Marr question remains well-posed after translation, and vice versa.
4. The four-layer test is either identical to this map or cleanly functorial over it.

(1) fails because Marr-L3 (realization) is not Hub-ontology, and Marr-L1 (function of a system) is not Hub-description of the cosmos. (2) fails because universe-ontology is not a realization slot: there is not “the same universe, many ontologies, all fine.” (3) fails because “what problem is vision solving?” does not remain well-posed as “what problem is the universe solving?” (4) fails because rung 4 is a *claim at the ontology layer*, which Marr never makes. `analysis`

A weaker, accurate claim:

> There is a **homomorphism of anti-smuggling constraints**: both frameworks forbid answering a higher/other question with a lower/other answer. The homomorphism is not invertible. `analysis`

That is the most that should be said in public without a hedge.

## 4. Where “representation / 表征” lives

Do not let one English (or Chinese) word occupy three jobs. Split:

| Sense | Home | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1. Account-representation** | Description | The theorist’s / agent’s compression: maps, invariants, models | Not what the system uses internally; not what the world is |
| **R2. System-representation** | Mechanism (Marr L2) | The encoding an algorithm transforms; the “representation for input and output” | Not the computational-level problem statement; not ontology |
| **R3. Ontological-representation** | Ontology, and almost always illicit | The claim that the world *is* a representation (of what? to whom?) | Not licensed by R1 or R2 |

v0 put “representational” in the description / computational cell. That is R1, and it is a real job. It is **not** Marr’s “representation.” Marr’s word is R2. Collapsing R1 with R2 is how “we have a good description” starts to sound like “the system computes that description,” and then like “the world is that description.” `analysis`

The personal-physics articulation already had the better three-way split in engineering language: 算法层面 / 表征层面 / 物理 substrate — with 表征 there meaning Hub-and-doctrine state (R1/R2 mixture), not “the universe is a picture.” At the engineering layer that vocabulary has working power. At ultimate-ontology it does not. `sediment`

The June 2026 intelligence-theory panel already recorded the Marr-shaped version of this error: selling an implementational identity (“memory = the dynamics itself”) as a computational theory; untagged X = Y identities are vacuous. `sediment`

**Working rule:** when a sentence contains 表征 / representation, tag it R1, R2, or R3 before it is allowed to travel. Untagged, it is not a term of art. `analysis`

## 5. Does universe-mainline need a fourth “observer / readout” level?

**No as a fourth ontological level. Yes as already-standing discipline.** `analysis`

Why Marr looks like it needs an observer: his computational level is defined relative to a viewer. Vision is a system *for* producing a useful description. Readout is not optional in that setting; it is the point of the system.

Why the universe does not inherit that slot:

1. **No external consumer.** Promoting observer/readout to a layer of *being* imports a vision-architecture into cosmology (idealist / simulation / anthropic slide). That is a new entity and must pay the explanation-gain bar. It has not. `analysis`
2. **The job is already staffed.** Observer split = physical interaction / perceptual compression / cognitive interpretation / linguistic report. That is an *epistemic* 4-tuple, not a fourth kind of substance. `sediment`
3. **Readout is a mechanism-layer coordinate.** It is the fourth entry of the render 4-tuple. Incomplete 4-tuple ⇒ metaphor. That is how to keep readout, not by making it ontology. `sediment`
4. **Readout substitution is a known failure mode, not a missing foundation.** Any system with state `s`, coupling `K`, and readout `H(s,K)` can be rewritten as an enlarged state `z = (s, K)` with identical trajectories and readouts. Calling a variable “readout” rather than “hidden state” is not identifiable without a declared partition and an intervention. Matched readout level/margin also fails to identify a self-boundary (ordinary multistability counterexample). `sediment`
5. **If information-as-ontology needs a reader, that is a mark against it, not a missing Marr level.** Either “information” has been redefined as structure-in-a-medium (rung 3, no reader), or the ontology has smuggled an observer. Those are different claims. Do not hide the fork inside a “fourth level.” `analysis`

If a fourth *something* is wanted, name it correctly: **epistemic / account layer** (how a claim is known and reported). That is already description + observer split. Do not add it to the ontology inventory. `analysis`

Consciousness, perception, and agency remain the right *subdomains* in which Marr’s observer-relative computational level is at home. They do not get to set the mainline’s layer-count. `sediment`

## 7. What to keep (the part that should become standing)

1. **Anti-smuggling as the shared primitive.** Not the 3×3. `analysis`
2. **Unbundle ontology from medium.** Marr L3 ↔ medium. Ontology stays the mainline question with no Marr image. `analysis`
3. **Tag 表征 as R1 / R2 / R3.** Default home of Marr’s “representation” is R2 ⊂ mechanism. `analysis`
4. **Keep the four-layer test orthogonal.** It is how informationalist claims are graded, not a fourth Marr level and not a rename of the Hub stack. `sediment` + `analysis`
5. **Observer / readout stays discipline, not inventory.** Observer split + 4-tuple readout + “exclude readout substitution first.” `sediment`
6. **Universe-mainline remains ontology.** Marr is native to vision / intelligence / agency subdomains. Exporting it upward is X2. `sediment`
7. **STT hygiene.** “Massey” → David Marr. 物理 and 实现 are one level, not two. 表征 is not the computational level. `analysis`
